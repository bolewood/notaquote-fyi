SECTION 38-73-425. Absence of credit information.

Absence of credit information may be used by an insurer for underwriting purposes only if the insurer presents information satisfactory to the director that the absence is related to the risk.

HISTORY: 2004 Act No. 290, SECTION 6, eff July 29, 2004.
