Education In South Carolina, insurers may use the highest level of education achieved as a rating factor. In this rating factor, for example, those with a college degree may pay less. Insurance Credit Score South Carolina state law allows insurers to use an insurance credit score as a rating factor. An insurance credit score is a number based on various financial, credit and risk factors. Some companies offer discounts to consumers who have higher insurance credit scores.
